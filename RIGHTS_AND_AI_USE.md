# Copyright, reuse, research and AI disclosure

**Intended licensing for material under the depositor's control:** CC BY 4.0 for original copyrightable text and figures; MIT for original code. No obligation for downstream users to publish modified versions; commercial reuse is permitted subject to applicable rights.

**Not automatically covered:** original Zenodo files whose recorded license has not been checked, third-party data/images/code, reports authored by other people, and material on which the depositor cannot confer rights. A human rights audit is required before including any such file under the proposed licenses. Preserve author names, citations, licenses and original Zenodo DOI.

**AI:** The research was AI-assisted, including potentially extensive mathematical derivation, code, bibliography and writing. AI-generated technical reviews do not constitute human peer review. Revalidate statements about human contributions against source records; do not invent external validation.
