# Description draft

This technical note consolidates the Large-Radius Local Flatness branch of the *Geometria e tempo emergente* project.

It formalizes a conservative geometric statement: local measurements can be insensitive to global curvature when the local scale L is much smaller than a curvature or horizon scale R, with corrections of order O((L/R)^2). The note separates local flatness, observational flatness, global flatness, topology, observable radius, and total universe size.

A model-dependent flat-Lambda-CDM calculation gives an illustrative observable radius of about 46.13 Gly and observable diameter of about 92.27 Gly for declared Planck-like parameters. The Big Bang is rejected as a spatial center, and horizon chaining is retained only as a conditional thought experiment.

This work does not propose a new gravity theory, does not claim a measurement of the total size of the Universe, does not infer global topology, and does not derive dark matter or dark energy. The earlier membrane branch and the separate navigator/software branch are not included in this publication.
